# NUTS 2024 lookup-table provenance

`nuts-2024.csv` is an offline lookup table used to turn EURES NUTS location
codes such as `DE724` into human-readable regions. It contains no boundary
geometry or representative coordinates. A recognised region name can be
passed separately to BetterOption's existing offline place index for rough
Explore ordering; it is never treated as an exact workplace or routing point.

## Sources and transformation

- Region codes and names: Annex I of Commission Delegated Regulation (EU)
  2023/674, which established the NUTS 2024 classification by amending
  Regulation (EC) No 1059/2003.
- Consolidated NUTS regulation applicable from 1 January 2024:
  <https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2003/1059/2024-01-01/eng>
- NUTS 2024 amending act and classification table:
  <https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg_del/2023/674/oj/eng>
- NUTS description and hierarchy:
  <https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/nuts>

Generated on 2026-08-03. The table is a compact machine-readable extraction of
the legal classification. It includes the 27 EU Member States' NUTS 0-3 rows;
non-EU statistical-region codes are intentionally not inferred. The European
Commission and Eurostat have not reviewed or endorsed this extraction.

## Reuse conditions and attribution

The European Commission reuse policy is implemented by Decision 2011/833/EU.
Eurostat's general reuse notice authorises commercial and non-commercial reuse
of statistical data when the source is acknowledged and modifications are
identified. This file does not contain or derive from GISCO boundary geometry,
so GISCO's separate non-commercial geographical-data restriction and the
EuroGeographics boundary notice do not attach to this lookup table.
