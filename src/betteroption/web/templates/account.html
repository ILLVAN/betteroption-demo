Privacy boundary

What belongs to you

  • Compass answers, extracted facts, corrections, and conversation history.
  • CV-derived proposals you chose to confirm. New alpha uploads do not retain the original file or transient selected CV text.
  • Saved-job access, match runs, feedback, application states, commute estimates, and background tasks.
  • Public job-advertisement source records remain shared corpus data and contain no private profile.

The download stays readable: account details, persona settings, and each persona’s current Compass. Operational task, provider, match, and revision histories remain available for secure diagnostics but are not included in this file.
