AI notice

Where AI assists—and where it does not decide.

This engineering-draft explanation covers Compass, CV, semantic match evidence, salary-research and application-drafting use. Point-of-use notices still matter, and this text requires external review before an alpha opens to other people.

Compass extraction

Your written Compass answer is sent to the configured OpenAI API. The model proposes structured preferences and experience statements. The proposal can be incomplete or wrong. You can inspect, correct or remove it, and only user-controlled confirmed statements affect candidate-side matching.

Optional CV extraction

The original file is parsed on WorkLifeBalance's server and is not sent to OpenAI. Identifiers are removed locally and apparent prohibited sensitive categories are screened. You see and may edit the exact selected text before a separate action sends it to OpenAI. You can decline and enter experience manually instead.

What is sent

For Compass: the text of the current answer and the section being completed. For optional CV processing: only the displayed experience, education, skills, tools, responsibilities and work-result text—not the original file, filename, contact details or exact address. For optional semantic match evidence: only criteria that the deterministic matcher could not resolve for that job, plus canonical public job facts and their short source quotes. Account, persona, raw Compass answers, CV text, private ratings, home location, provider URLs and the complete advertisement are excluded.

Semantic match evidence

For a bounded number of jobs, OpenAI may classify whether supplied public evidence satisfies, supports, contradicts or leaves unresolved one supplied confirmed criterion. It may cite only supplied evidence identifiers and cannot set a score or recommendation category. Deterministic comparisons take priority. A low-confidence contradiction remains unknown; only a validated medium-confidence contradiction can become an automatic blocker. The assessment and its evidence references are stored with the match so later private rating or evidence corrections can update the score locally without another model call. AI-assisted reasons are labelled in Job Detail.

Public application guidance

WorkLifeBalance's deterministic application layer may identify a source-published business contact person, role, email, phone number, postal address, application channel, requested documents, limits, deadline or instruction only from the job advertisement or employer's official careers page supplied as the job source. This processing runs locally or on the operator-controlled application server, not in an LLM, and no configured model receives the extracted contact fields. A name remains personal data even when published for applications. WorkLifeBalance keeps the job binding, source and unknown state visible, does not search private profiles or unrelated sources, and never contacts the person automatically. A safe salutation is constructed locally from an exact source-published form of address or your explicit choice; WorkLifeBalance never infers gender from a name. This feature remains local-only unless the external-alpha Article 6(1)(f) legitimate-interest assessment, Article 14 concept, provider, retention and deletion review is complete.

Optional salary orientation

When salary research is enabled, you have an active compensation criterion, and a job advertisement states no pay, WorkLifeBalance may research a bounded set of those jobs. It sends OpenAI Responses web search only public job fields needed for the research: title, country or coarse job location, public job summary, and limited public role, experience, employment or weekly-hours facts. The research adapter never receives your compensation criterion, salary target, Compass/profile facts, persona name, account details, home location or other candidate data.

The research is country-aware and currently accepts evidence only from its explicit official EU, national employment/statistics and collective-agreement source policy, plus an attributable European Central Bank source when currency conversion is necessary. No secondary salary source is currently enabled; adding one later requires prior methodological and provider/source review and corroboration. The result records its country, original currency, pay period and source links. A German benchmark is never extrapolated to another country, and WorkLifeBalance shows no estimate when the country, occupation mapping or evidence is not adequately supported.

Every estimate is a cautious market orientation, not pay advertised by the employer and not a hard matching blocker. Source pages may receive ordinary web-search or page-retrieval request data through OpenAI's search service. This optional path must remain local-only until the exact OpenAI search configuration, source policy, contracts, transfers, retention and notices have passed the external-alpha review.

Optional cover-letter drafting

When you press a create or rewrite control, WorkLifeBalance sends the selected public job's relevant facts and evidence, text-relevant application rules and only minimised confirmed Compass facts needed to draft for that role. Contact names, identifying roles, email addresses, phone numbers, postal addresses and delivery URLs remain inside WorkLifeBalance and are removed from every drafting-prompt surface. Private employer ratings and notes are not prompt material. The model is instructed not to infer a person's identity, role, honorific or gender. WorkLifeBalance adds a locally constructed neutral greeting or a greeting based on your explicit choice after generation. Lengthening or shortening also sends the current draft after the same contact-data minimisation. The model is instructed not to invent qualifications. Review and edit every result: automated minimisation and the draft can still be inaccurate or unsuitable.

The editable result is stored in the active persona's workspace for up to 180 days after its last update, unless you delete it earlier. WorkLifeBalance does not send the letter to an employer, submit an application or make a decision for you.

Provider handling

OpenAI API data is not used to train OpenAI models by default unless the operator opts in; this alpha must not opt in and does not use real profiles for its own training or evaluation. Default API abuse-monitoring logs may contain prompts and outputs for up to 30 days unless an approved shorter-retention control applies. Salary-research source sites have their own technical logs and policies and must be included in the source and transfer review before external use.

Private employer context

A private employer rating or short research note may be used as your candidate-side matching signal only after the employer has been identified uniquely enough to avoid mixing unrelated companies. Placeholder employers such as “Unknown” cannot receive shared employer context. The note stays private to the active persona, is not published and is not sent to an LLM.

Separate public-vacancy prototype

An approved local job-poster account may enter a public vacancy through a separate interface. Publication itself does not send candidate data to the poster or to an AI provider. The poster cannot choose, score or inspect an audience, and receives no candidate profiles, ratings, matches, clicks or drafts. Poster-supplied business contact fields remain local or on the operator-controlled server and are not added to Compass, salary-research or cover-letter prompts.

After publication, WorkLifeBalance can treat that vacancy like other public-job evidence in candidate-side search and matching. The result is shown only to the candidate; there is no employer-side candidate ranking. Hosted publication remains disabled until the poster, data-protection, Digital Services Act and AI Act launch reviews are complete.

Matching and the EU AI Act boundary

WorkLifeBalance's matching compares your confirmed criteria with public job evidence and returns recommendations only to you. A narrow AI-assisted relation can resolve otherwise unknown evidence, but deterministic code applies confidence rules, blocker policy and every score. It does not rank or filter candidates for employers, decide who receives employment, give posters or employers access to candidate scores, or submit applications. Scores and salary orientations are aids, not guarantees; an estimated salary remains unknown evidence rather than a pass or fail.

Annex III point 4(a) of the EU AI Act covers AI systems intended for recruitment or selection, including placing targeted job advertisements, analysing or filtering applications and evaluating candidates. The lack of poster targeting controls and employer-side candidate access is a deliberate boundary, but it does not by itself settle whether candidate-side ranking that includes first-party vacancies falls outside Annex III. Intended purpose, contractual positioning, UI, actual use and any future employer influence must be assessed together. This is not a completed legal classification. A documented classification memorandum, qualified review and updated AI transparency assessment remain launch gates before hosted vacancy publication or an external alpha.

Your choices

Do not enter special-category or criminal-offence data. Review every proposed statement, job source and application draft. You can correct, export and delete information from the service. Optional CV processing and cover-letter generation are not required for ordinary use.
