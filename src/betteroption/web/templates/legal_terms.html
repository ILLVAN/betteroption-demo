Eligibility and access

Local loopback development may use open registration for test accounts. The supported external private alpha is free, invitation-only and for adults aged 18 or older. Invitations are personal, time-limited and may not be transferred. Keep account and one-time-link credentials confidential and report suspected compromise promptly.

Candidate-only matching scope

WorkLifeBalance helps you describe work preferences and experience, find and add public job links, compare evidence, prepare editable cover-letter drafts and track your own decisions. It does not provide a poster, employer or recruiter access to profiles, personas, rankings, ratings, matches or drafts; rank or filter candidates for an employer; submit an application; contact an employer; or guarantee an interview or employment. Candidate matching is separate from the public-vacancy prototype below.

Separate verified job-poster prototype

In local development, an account may request limited permission to publish vacancies for a named organisation. The operator may require a work email, company website/domain and evidence that the requester is authorised to act for that organisation. Approval is discretionary, revocable and not a guarantee of identity, authority or vacancy authenticity. Hosted publication remains unavailable until the qualified legal, data-protection and platform review and the operational controls listed below are complete.

A published vacancy becomes public information available to any visitor and may enter the same candidate-side search corpus as other public jobs. A poster receives no candidate data or analytics and cannot choose which candidates see, rank or open it.

Job-poster rules

A poster must publish only a genuine, current and legally offerable vacancy for an organisation it is authorised to represent. The employer identity, role, material working conditions, location or remote status, application route and deadline must be accurate and kept current. The poster must promptly correct or withdraw a closed or inaccurate vacancy and may include a named person or business contact only where authorised to publish it for that application purpose.

Fake, deceptive, duplicate, discriminatory or unlawful vacancies; impersonation; phishing; hidden fees; advance-payment or credential-harvesting schemes; malicious links or files; misleading salary or employment claims; unauthorised third-party personal data; special-category or criminal-offence data; and content that infringes another person's rights are prohibited. A poster must comply with employment, equal-treatment, advertising and accessibility rules applicable to the vacancy. By submitting content, the poster grants the operator only the non-exclusive permission needed to store, review, display, index, correct formatting, moderate and withdraw that vacancy while it is active.

AI and evidence limitations

OpenAI-assisted extraction, optional salary research and drafting, and public-web evidence can be incomplete, outdated or wrong. Matching scores, summaries, salary orientations, application contacts/process instructions and application drafts are experimental aids for you, not factual guarantees, professional advice or automated employment decisions. Source-published application contacts are processed locally or on the operator-controlled server and excluded from LLM prompts; a safe salutation is constructed locally without inferring gender from a name. A researched salary range is not pay advertised by the employer, is not a hard matching blocker and may be unavailable where country-aware evidence is insufficient. Review your Compass and every generated or edited letter, and verify each advertisement, employer, contractual entity, contact person/address, salary, route, working condition, deadline and application document with primary sources.

Private employer context

Your employer rating and research note stay private to the active persona, are not sent to an LLM and may be attached only to a sufficiently unique employer identity. Do not add confidential information about another person. Placeholder employers such as “Unknown” cannot safely share ratings or notes across jobs.

Prohibited uploads and use

Do not provide another person's confidential data; health/disability, ethnicity, religion, political views, union membership, genetic/biometric, sex-life/orientation, criminal-offence, background-check or security-clearance data; unlawful, infringing or malicious content; or information you are not authorised to use. Do not automate applications, impersonate another person, bypass access controls/rate limits, scrape the service, attack systems, or violate source-provider terms.

Your control and responsibility

You decide what confirmed information affects recommendations, whether to request a draft and whether to use or change any result. WorkLifeBalance never submits a draft automatically. You remain responsible for checking every statement and for every application and communication. Optional CV processing and application drafting can be declined; ordinary Compass entry remains available. Salary research uses only public job fields and never your salary target or profile data; if the sources cannot support the job's country and occupation, the service should show no estimate rather than substitute another country's benchmark.

Availability and change

The alpha may be paused, restricted, reset or discontinued to protect users, meet legal/security obligations, repair defects or change experimental features. A vacancy can be de-listed when it closes, appears unsafe or unlawful, cannot be verified, breaches these rules, or the poster's access is withdrawn. There is no service-level commitment. Material term changes receive a new version and, where required, renewed acceptance.

Moderation, notices and review

Before hosted vacancy publication, the operator must provide an accessible electronic route for anyone to report a specific allegedly illegal vacancy, preserve the information needed to assess it, decide diligently and without arbitrariness, and communicate a clear reason where the Digital Services Act requires one. A poster must cooperate with verification and moderation and may not republish removed content to evade a decision. The hosted service must define a correction or review route for moderation decisions where required.

Public dissemination of poster-supplied vacancies may make the service a hosting service or online platform under the DSA. Articles 14, 16 and 17 therefore require specific review even if the operator is a micro or small enterprise. Article 19 may exempt a qualifying micro or small online platform from some additional Section 3 duties, potentially including Article 20's internal complaint system, but no exemption or classification is promised. The operator must document both before launch.

Suspension

Candidate or poster access may be suspended for security risk, prohibited use, legal requirements, invitation abuse, false authority claims, unsafe vacancies or material breach. Publication access may be revoked and active vacancies de-listed. The operator should give a reason and a reasonable correction path where safe and legally possible and where required by law.

Privacy and retention for posters

Job-poster verification and moderation use account, work-contact, employer-identity, authority and decision records. Published vacancy fields are public. Posters must read the Privacy Notice and must not submit another person's business contact unless authorised and the relevant GDPR transparency route is available. Withdrawn or removed vacancies stop being public; the underlying vacancy, verification, moderation and backup records require a documented, enforced retention and deletion schedule before hosted use.

Legal completion required

Before any external candidate alpha, the operator must complete the Article 6(1)(f) legitimate-interest and Articles 13/14 contact-transparency records, the intended-purpose and EU AI Act classification memorandum, processor and transfer reviews, retention/deletion verification and qualified legal review. Before hosted vacancy publication it must additionally complete the controller-role and lawful-basis assessment for posters and business contacts; poster identity/authority verification; fraud, discrimination and content rules; DSA service classification; Article 14 terms; Article 16 notice/action; Article 17 reasoned decisions; any applicable Article 20 complaint route; moderation records; and a practical correction, objection and removal process.

Because Annex III point 4(a) of the EU AI Act includes AI used to place targeted job advertisements, counsel must assess the intended purpose and actual operation of candidate-side ranking that includes first-party vacancies. No high-risk/non-high-risk conclusion is made in these draft terms. German/EU counsel must also complete mandatory consumer and business-customer information, statutory warranty/liability language, governing law/venue, dispute-resolution information, business identity and termination consequences for the real operator. No placeholder or blanket liability exclusion is intended to override mandatory rights.
