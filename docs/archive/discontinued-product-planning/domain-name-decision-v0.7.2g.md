# Domain and product-name decision — v0.7.2g

Status: naming judgement only. No product, UI, deployment, logo, email identity,
or canonical-domain change is part of this release.

## Decision

Keep `betteroption.example` as the canonical address through the private alpha.
The six newly supplied names add two credible ideas, but none beats the clarity
of the current domain or the two existing challengers.

Updated top six:

1. `betteroption.example` — canonical alpha name.
2. `balancedwork.life` — strongest clear and practical challenger.
3. `lifelife.work` — strongest distinctive and emotional challenger.
4. `movingon.work` — best new candidate, but framed around leaving or transition.
5. `geta.life` — memorable campaign language, not yet a reassuring product name.
6. `milesahead.work` — energetic, but too close to existing recruitment usage.

## Full comparison

Prices are the Cloudflare figures supplied by the project owner on 5 August

| 9 | `workwork.life` | $28.20 | Memorable symmetry | Doubles work and puts it before life | Reject |

The previously reviewed lower tier remains unchanged: `easyjobsearch.life` is
descriptive but generic, `easyjobs.work` and `easyjobs.live` are too close to
the active `easy.jobs` recruiting product, `welcom.work` looks misspelled,
`lifelife.cc` weakens trust, and `lifelifebalance.life` is too long and
repetitive.

## Why the new options do not change the decision

`movingon.work` is the only new name that adds a genuinely different, coherent
story. That story is transition rather than balance, so it narrows the product
at exactly the point where the current name remains clear. `milesahead.work`
has better energy but a worse sector-collision signal. The other four are less
clear, more local than the product, or already too close to a job-market name.

This is a product scan, not trademark or company-name clearance. Any future
rename still requires domain-history, company-name, trademark, social-handle,
email, pronunciation, and renewal-price checks in the intended launch markets.

No naming-related application name, static UI copy, logo, canonical-domain, or
external brand-link change is included in `v0.7.2g`.
