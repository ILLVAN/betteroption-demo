# Domain-name decision — v0.7.2i

Status: product-risk desk review dated 5 August 2026. This is not trademark,
company-name, domain-history, social-handle, or legal clearance. Availability
and prices below are the project owner's checkout snapshots and must be
confirmed before any purchase.

## Decision

Keep `betteroption.example` as the private-alpha identity. Do not rename the
product in `v0.7.2i` and do not buy any of the €199/year `.jobs` candidates.

`chilled.work` is the only new candidate worth a small blind test. It is short,
memorable, inexpensive at the supplied $10.20 snapshot, and supports the calm
work-life voice. Its real weakness is equally clear: “chilled” can sound lazy,
unserious, or refrigerated. It therefore earns a test slot, not a rename or an
automatic purchase.

The current top six are:

1. `betteroption.example`
2. `suiting.work`
3. `chilled.work`
4. `lifelife.work`
5. `balancedwork.life`
6. `movingon.work`

`chilled.work` may be placed fourth rather than third in a very conservative
trust-weighted test. It does not displace the current canonical name.

Registrar email forwarding routes incoming mail. It is not a mailbox, outbound
business identity, transactional-email provider, or deployment feature, so it
does not improve the hosting case for the `.jobs` names.

## Purchase gate

If the owner still wants one inexpensive experiment, test
`betteroption.example`, `suiting.work`, and `chilled.work` blind with the same
landing-page copy. Measure unaided comprehension, trust, spoken-to-typed
accuracy, and 24-hour recall. A purchase still requires checkout confirmation
of first-year price, renewal, transfer, restoration, VAT, auto-renewal, privacy,
and cancellation terms. A rename additionally requires professional clearance.

No UI, legal-name, package, repository, email, deployment, redirect, or
canonical-domain change is authorized by this note.
