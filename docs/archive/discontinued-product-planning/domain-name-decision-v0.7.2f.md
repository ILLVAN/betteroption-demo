# Domain and product-name decision — v0.7.2f

Status: naming judgement only. No product, UI, deployment or canonical-domain
rename is part of this release.

## Decision

Keep `betteroption.example` as the canonical address through the private alpha.
It states the promise immediately, is already owned for three years, and avoids
spending product attention on redirects, email identity, legal notices and user
recognition before real-user naming evidence exists.

The updated shortlist has two credible challengers:

1. `balancedwork.life` — the strongest practical alternative. Read across the
   dot, it forms “balanced work life”; it is clear, calm and trustworthy, though
   less distinctive.
2. `lifelife.work` — the strongest personality alternative. It puts life first
   twice and is memorable on screen, but the doubled word is error-prone when
   spoken and can suggest postponing rather than improving work.

`geta.life` is excellent campaign language, but too sharp and too vague for the
primary trust brand of a product used during a potentially stressful job search.

## Collision signals, not trademark clearance

This was only a preliminary product scan. It is not legal clearance and says
nothing conclusive about registrability in relevant jurisdictions.

- Apple currently lists a separate **LifeLife** AI-powered life-simulator app:
  <https://apps.apple.com/us/app/lifelife/id6755062821>.
- **ALIVE @ WORK®** is used by an established leadership consultancy:
  <https://aliveatwork.com/>.
- **easy.jobs** is an active recruitment/ATS product, making both EasyJobs
  variants unnecessarily close to the same market:
  <https://easy.jobs/>.
- **Balanced Work Life** also has adjacent coaching usage:
  <https://balancedworklife.com/who-we-are/>.

These findings demote the alternatives; they do not automatically disqualify
them. Any real rename needs domain-history, company-name, trademark and social
handle checks in the intended launch markets.

## Low-risk validation

Before buying or renaming:

1. Verify first-year, renewal, transfer and premium-renewal prices separately.
2. Test `betteroption.example`, `balancedwork.life` and `lifelife.work` with people
   who have not seen the product.
3. Ask what each name promises before explaining the product.
4. Measure immediate spoken spelling and unaided recall one day later.
5. Ask whether the name feels supportive during a difficult job search—not only
   whether it sounds clever.
6. If a challenger wins clearly, use it first as a redirect and plan the product,
   legal, email and deployment rename as a separate release.

No naming-related application name, logo, canonical-domain or external
brand-link changes in `v0.7.2f`.
